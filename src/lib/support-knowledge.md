## About OfferIQ

OfferIQ is an all-in-one AI platform that helps creators, coaches, and online entrepreneurs build and launch high-converting sales funnels, landing pages, email sequences, and marketing campaigns — without needing a developer or copywriter.

The core idea: you describe your offer, and OfferIQ's AI analyzes it deeply and then generates your entire marketing system automatically — sales copy, funnel pages, email sequences, and traffic strategy.

---

## How OfferIQ Works (The 3-Phase Flow)

### Phase 1: Offer Analysis
- Go to `/analyze` and fill in your offer details: product/service name, price, target audience, key benefits, and unique selling proposition.
- Click "Analyze My Offer" — the AI runs a deep analysis through two phases:
  - **Structural Analysis**: Offer scoring (0–100), funnel blueprint, pricing strategy, platform priority matrix
  - **Strategic Analysis**: Persona psychology, positioning, conversion hooks, messaging angles, monetization narrative
- You receive a full **Intelligence Report** with an Offer Score and actionable insights.

### Phase 2: AI System Generation
- From the Intelligence Report, click "Generate Complete System"
- The AI automatically creates:
  1. An optimized funnel structure
  2. Conversion-focused sales copy
  3. High-converting landing pages
  4. An automated email sequence
  5. Traffic intelligence recommendations
- You can pause and edit at any step.

### Phase 3: Launch & Optimization
- Review your system and make minor tweaks (headlines, colors, email timing, traffic budget)
- Connect your payment processor and email service
- Launch and monitor live metrics from your dashboard

## Features

### Funnel Builder
- Drag-and-drop landing page builder with pre-designed high-converting "Macro-Components" (Hero sections, Pricing sections, Testimonials, CTAs, etc.)
- AI-powered component editor: chat with the AI to change copy, colors, styles, and layout
- Publish pages to a custom domain or OfferIQ subdomain

### Intelligence Report
- Deep AI analysis of your offer across 10+ dimensions
- Offer score out of 100 with breakdown by category
- Pain point identification, persona psychology, positioning analysis
- Funnel blueprint tailored to your offer type

### Sales Copy Generator
- AI writes full sales page copy based on your Intelligence Report
- Covers: headline, sub-headline, benefits, objection handling, CTA, proof sections
- Can regenerate individual sections

### Email Sequence Builder
- AI builds a complete email nurture and sales sequence
- Each email has subject line, preview text, and body copy
- AI agent can rewrite, add, or delete emails on command

### Traffic Intelligence
- Platform priority matrix: shows which ad platforms suit your offer best
- Budget allocation recommendations
- Audience targeting suggestions per platform

### Workspaces
- Organize multiple offers/clients into separate workspaces
- Each workspace has its own pages, funnels, and email sequences
- Agency plan allows subaccounts with custom permissions

## Common Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Do I need any technical skills to use OfferIQ?**
A: No. OfferIQ is designed for non-technical users. The AI does the heavy lifting — you just describe your offer and answer a few questions. No coding, design, or copywriting skills required.

**Q: How long does it take to build a complete funnel?**
A: The AI-generated system (pages, copy, emails) is typically ready in under 5 minutes. Most users complete the full setup — including review and minor customizations — in 15 minutes.

**Q: Can I use my own domain?**
A: Yes. You can connect a custom domain to your published pages. Go to Settings → Domains and follow the DNS instructions.

**Q: What's the difference between the Funnel Builder and the Intelligence Report?**
A: The Intelligence Report is the analysis phase — it diagnoses your offer and creates a strategic blueprint. The Funnel Builder is the execution phase — it builds the actual pages based on that blueprint. The report comes first; everything flows from it.

**Q: Can I edit the AI-generated content?**
A: Yes, everything is editable. You can chat with the AI to make changes, or directly edit text in the builder. You're always in control.

**Q: Does OfferIQ handle payments?**
A: OfferIQ doesn't process payments directly. It integrates with Stripe, Paddle, or JVZoo — you connect your account and they handle all transactions.

**Q: Can I have multiple offers/funnels?**
A: Yes. Each Workspace can contain multiple campaigns. You can also create multiple Workspaces. The number of workspaces depends on your plan.

**Q: What is a Workspace?**
A: A Workspace is a container for one business or client. It holds all the pages, funnels, emails, and settings for that offer. Think of it like a project folder.

**Q: Can I give my team access?**
A: Yes, on Agency plan. You can create Subaccounts with custom permissions (view-only, edit, create, etc.).

**Q: How do I cancel my subscription?**
A: Go to Settings → Billing → Cancel Plan. You can also email support directly. See our Refund Policy at `/refund`.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
A: Yes. OfferIQ uses Supabase for secure data storage, Stripe/Paddle for PCI-compliant billing, and all connections are encrypted via HTTPS. See our Privacy Policy at `/policy`.

**Q: I got an error — what should I do?**
A: First, try refreshing the page. If the issue persists, try logging out and back in. If it's still broken, contact us at support (see below).

**Q: The AI generated something I don't like. Can I redo it?**
A: Yes! You can regenerate individual sections, or chat with the AI agent to request specific changes. The AI is collaborative — just tell it what you want differently.

**Q: Can I import an existing page or funnel?**
A: Currently, OfferIQ focuses on AI-generated content from your offer description. Import features are on the roadmap.

**Q: What AI models power OfferIQ?**
A: OfferIQ uses Anthropic's Claude models (Sonnet and Opus tier). The intelligence report uses the most powerful models for deep analysis; page editing and chat use faster models for speed.
